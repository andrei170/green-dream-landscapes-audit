Grounds Maintenance Is The Business. Sell It That Way.
The strategic finding, and the reason this audit is worth your time

Landscaping is a feast-and-famine trade. You build a garden for £12,000, you get paid, and then you need another one. Grounds maintenance is the opposite — a contract, a schedule, an invoice every month, and a customer who does not go away in January.

A one-off landscape build won once, paid once, then you start again A maintenance contract won once, paid every month, renews Where it sits on your site one of six equal menu items Its own dedicated page ✗ A commercial page at all ✗

The buyers are different people. A homeowner wants a garden that looks lovely. A schools trust, a housing association, a managing agent or an industrial estate wants a reliable contractor with insurance and a schedule — and they are searching for exactly that. They arrive at your site, find a homeowner's landscaping page, and cannot tell whether you do what they need.

You are recruiting an experienced team leader. That is the shape of a firm adding capacity. Capacity paid for by contracts is a safer business than capacity paid for by the next big build.

Nothing Is Being Measured
We read your served page source directly
GA4 tag ✗ none Google Tag Manager ✗ none Universal Analytics ✗ none Meta pixel ✗ none Conversion tracking ✗ none

Six services, no data. So nobody can say whether the enquiries are coming for tree surgery or for design and build, which page produces them, or what any of it costs. With six services and no measurement, effort gets spread evenly across all six by default — when the honest likelihood is that one or two of them pay for the business.

This is first, it is cheap, and it has to happen before any advertising is worth running: Google's bidding needs conversion data to optimise against, and with none it optimises for clicks.

Three Testimonials And No Number
The proof is real but it is not in a form that competes

You have three named testimonials with genuine local places attached — Fixby, Brighouse and Leeds. That is real proof and it is more than many have.

What is missing is a star rating and a review count. When someone chooses a landscaper for a £10,000 garden, they compare three firms and the visible number does the deciding. Paragraphs of praise on your own website are read as marketing; a rating out of five with a count beside it is read as evidence, because the customer knows you did not write it.

Nothing on this page is cheaper to fix. It costs a message to recent customers.

Fix First — No Spend Required
  • Install GA4 and conversion tracking. Six services and no data is the core problem
  • Make 07534 189761 a tel: link on every page
  • Rewrite the title tag. Drop “Home”, add landscaping, grounds maintenance and Huddersfield
  • Ask recent customers for a Google review so the proof becomes a number, not a paragraph
Build Next
  • A dedicated grounds maintenance page written for a facilities or estate manager, not a homeowner
  • A separate commercial route in — insurance, schedules, references, and a form that asks site size and frequency
  • Publish insurance and any APL or BALI membership if you hold them — commercial buyers check first
  • Town pages for Huddersfield, Brighouse, Halifax, Elland, Holmfirth and Dewsbury

Six services, no measurement, and the recurring one buried among the rest.

The landscaping is clearly good — the photographs and the testimonials show that. The gap is that the part of your business that could pay you every month is presented as an afterthought.
